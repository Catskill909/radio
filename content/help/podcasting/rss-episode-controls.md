# RSS Episode Controls & Archiving

Control how many episodes appear in your RSS feeds and manage archived recordings.

## Episode Limit Settings

When editing a show, you'll find **RSS Feed Settings** with two controls:

### Episodes in Feed
Set how many episodes appear in your podcast RSS feed:
- **Preset buttons**: Quick options for 2, 5, 10, 20, 30, 50, or 100 episodes
- **∞ (Unlimited)**: All episodes appear in the feed (default)
- **Custom**: Enter any number you need

> **Tip**: The "2" button includes a music note (♪) as a reminder for music shows that may require limited episode counts for licensing compliance.

### Archiving Toggle
Choose what happens to episodes beyond the feed limit:

- **Keep old episodes** (Archiving ON): Episodes beyond the limit stay on disk but don't appear in RSS. They can be downloaded or deleted from Settings → Archive Management.

- **Delete old episodes** (Archiving OFF): When a new recording completes, the oldest episodes beyond the limit are automatically deleted (both audio file and database record).

> ⚠️ **Warning**: Auto-delete is permanent. If archiving is OFF, audio files cannot be recovered. Download files you want to keep from Settings → Audio before enabling auto-delete.

## Visual Stats

Podcast cards display episode stats as badges:
- **Blue badge**: "3 in feed" shows how many episodes are in your RSS
- **Gray text**: "· 2 archived" shows how many are kept on disk but not in RSS

## Archive Management

In **Settings → Archive Management**, you can:

1. **View archives by show**: See which shows have archived episodes and total storage used
2. **Expand episode lists**: Click a show to see individual archived episodes with dates and file sizes
3. **Download single episodes**: Download individual audio files
4. **Download All (ZIP)**: Download all archived episodes for a show as a ZIP file with metadata.json
5. **Delete episodes**: Remove individual episodes or clear all archives for a show

### Bulk Delete Confirmation
Deleting all archives for a show requires typing "DELETE" to confirm. This prevents accidental data loss.
