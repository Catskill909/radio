# Site Branding

Customize how your station appears on the public listen page (`/listen`).

## Settings

### Site Title
The name of your station displayed prominently in the listen page header. This is separate from your Station Name (used in RSS feeds) to allow for different branding on the web vs. podcast apps.

### Site Tagline
A short slogan or description that appears below your site title. Use this to communicate your station's identity or current programming focus.

**Examples:**
- "Your Community Radio Station"
- "The Best in Underground Music"
- "News, Talk & Entertainment"

### Site Logo
Upload a logo image to display in the listen page header. For best results:
- Use a square or horizontal image
- PNG with transparency works well
- Recommended size: 200-400px wide

## Preview
Changes are previewed in real-time before you save. The preview shows exactly how your branding will appear to listeners.

## Tips
- Keep your tagline short and memorable
- Test how your logo looks on both desktop and mobile
- Consider how your branding works with the dark theme
