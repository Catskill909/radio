# Station Settings

The Settings page is your central hub for configuring station-wide preferences. All settings here affect how StationDock operates and how your station appears to listeners.

## Available Settings

### Site Branding
Customize how your station appears on the public listen page:
- **Site Title** – The name displayed in the header
- **Site Tagline** – A short description or slogan
- **Site Logo** – Your station's visual identity

### Custom Menu
Configure the floating action menu on your public listen page with custom links and modal content.

### Station Identity
Set your station's core metadata used in podcast feeds and public pages:
- Station name
- Description
- Contact email
- Default artwork

### Station Timezone
Select the timezone for your station's operations. This affects:
- Schedule display
- Recording start/stop times
- The live clock display

### Station Audio Stream
Choose which Icecast stream plays on your public listen page.

### Audio Encoding Quality
Configure how recordings are encoded:
- Codec (MP3, AAC, Opus, FLAC)
- Bitrate and sample rate
- VBR/CBR mode

### Data Management
Import and export your station's shows, schedules, and images for backup or migration.
