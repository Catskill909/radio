# Station Identity

Configure your station's core metadata. This information is used in podcast RSS feeds and throughout the application.

## Settings

### Station Name
The official name of your radio station. This appears in:
- Podcast feed metadata
- Browser page titles
- Email notifications

### Station Description
A description of your station and its programming. This is included in your podcast RSS feeds and helps listeners understand what your station offers.

**Tips for a good description:**
- Mention your music genres or talk show topics
- Include your broadcast location if relevant
- Keep it under 200 words for best compatibility

### Contact Email
The primary contact email for your station. Used as:
- The owner email in podcast feeds
- Reply-to address for any system notifications

### Station Logo / Default Artwork
Upload your station's logo. This image serves as:
- The default artwork for shows without their own cover image
- The channel artwork in podcast feeds
- A fallback for any missing images

**Recommended specifications:**
- Square aspect ratio (1400x1400 pixels ideal for podcast directories)
- JPEG or PNG format
- File size under 2MB
